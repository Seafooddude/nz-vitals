About & methodology

What this is

NZ Vitals tracks a handful of headline indicators of the New Zealand economy on one page. It updates automatically when official data is released.

Indicators

For each indicator, write a short paragraph here: what it measures, why it matters, and how to read it. This is the part of the site that shows your economics thinking.

Sources

  • Stats NZ: CPI, unemployment, GDP
  • Reserve Bank of New Zealand: OCR, exchange rate, bond yields

Limitations

Data is revised by the source agencies. This site always shows the latest published series. It is not financial advice.
